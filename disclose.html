How this page works

Clean Then Guard is a short checklist for Atlanta homeowners. It copies phone numbers that appear on each shop's own website. It does not invent prices, licenses, or service areas.

The customer never pays this directory. No listing fee is charged to the shops in the first four outreach touches.

Governing law for any dispute about this page is the law of the State of Georgia, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. First metro is Atlanta.

If a number changes or a shop asks to be removed, reply to the listing email and the public city page is updated.
